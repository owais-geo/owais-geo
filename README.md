### Hi, I'm Mohammad Owais

I am a **Geology graduate** (`BSc Hons. Geology`, Cluster University of Jammu) working at the intersection of **hydrogeology, groundwater quality assessment, and satellite remote sensing** across the NW Himalayas and Jammu piedmont–alluvial plains.

My work focuses on turning field hydrogeology and open earth-observation archives into reproducible **Python** and **Google Earth Engine** workflows.
